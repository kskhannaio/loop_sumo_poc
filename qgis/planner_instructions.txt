MADIWALA LOOP - STUDY MAP AND PROPOSALS
=======================================

You need QGIS (free): https://qgis.org

OPEN IT
1. Unzip this folder anywhere. Keep the files in it together and don't rename them yet.
2. Double-click  madiwala.qgz  - QGIS opens on the Madiwala loop.

EXPLORE THE STUDY (same as the study website)
- Hover to see what anything is: turn on map tips once with  View > Show Map Tips  (or the speech-bubble
  icon in the toolbar), then hold the mouse over a bus stop, a crossing, a shop, a footpath dot...
- Switch views like the website's tabs: click the eye icon at the top of the Layers panel and pick
  Overview, Crossing, Buses, Footpaths, Street, Site check - or Draw proposals (the starting view).
- Read the story, figures and checklist: in the group "Story, figures and checklist (tables)",
  right-click a table > Open Attribute Table.
- Satellite view: tick "Satellite" in the Background group.

PROPOSE CHANGES
3. In the Layers panel, click "Proposed changes (points)" - or "(lines)" for things along a road.
4. Click the pencil (Toggle Editing), then "Add Point Feature" (or "Add Line Feature").
5. Click on the map where the change goes. A form opens: choose what the change is from the list,
   add details if they apply, and a short note on why.
   For lines, click along the road and right-click to finish.
6. Repeat for each change. Optionally fill in "About this proposal" (open its attribute table,
   click the pencil, add one row).
7. Click the pencil again and choose Save.
8. Email back the file  madiwala_proposals.gpkg  (rename it with your name first if you like).

Everything except your proposal layers is study data and is locked. The purple squares are the
study's own 8 proposed crossings, shown as an example of a finished proposal.
