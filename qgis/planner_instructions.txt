MADIWALA LOOP - PROPOSING CHANGES
=================================

You need QGIS (free): https://qgis.org

1. Unzip this folder anywhere. Keep the two files in it together and don't rename them yet.
2. Double-click  madiwala.qgz  - QGIS opens on the Madiwala loop.
3. In the Layers panel, click "Proposed changes (points)" (or "(lines)" for things along a road).
4. Click the pencil (Toggle Editing), then "Add Point Feature" (or "Add Line Feature").
5. Click on the map where the change goes. A form opens: choose what the change is from the list,
   add details if they apply, and a short note on why.
   For lines, click along the road and right-click to finish.
6. Repeat for each change. Optionally fill in "About this proposal" (open its attribute table).
7. Click the pencil again and choose Save.
8. Email back the file  madiwala_proposals.gpkg  (rename it with your name first if you like).

Everything except your two "Proposed changes" layers is study data and is locked. Tick layers on
and off in the Layers panel: the crossings study, the flyover contract (ramp wall, footpath
survey), buses, places of interest and walking distances. The purple squares are the study's own
8 proposed crossings, shown as an example of a finished proposal.
